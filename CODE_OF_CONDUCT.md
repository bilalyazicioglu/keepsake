# Code of conduct

Keepsake follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, keep feedback about the work, and don't harass anyone in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer, [@bilalyazicioglu](https://github.com/bilalyazicioglu), privately. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, lock threads, or block people who break these rules, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
